# MLOG-2025-014 ETCH-03-B: Reflected power high after quarterly PM

Date: 2025-03-11. Chamber: ETCH-03-B.

## Symptom

Two days after the quarterly PM, reflected RF power on ETCH-03-B climbed from 12 W to 23 W. Yield engineering reported edge ring failures on 6 lots run in chamber B; chamber A on the same tool was clean.

## Finding and action

Match capacitor C2 found not fully seated after the PM. Re-seated C2, recalibrated C1 and C2, ran auto-tune.

## Result

Reflected power back to 11.8 W. Seasoning plus 2 test wafers passed; edge yield matched chamber A. Chamber released after 9 hours down.
