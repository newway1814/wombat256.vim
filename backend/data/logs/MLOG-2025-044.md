# MLOG-2025-044 IMP-02-A: Sheet resistance drift on well implant

Date: 2025-07-01. Chamber: IMP-02-A.

## Symptom

Monitor wafer sheet resistance 3.1 percent high; parametric threshold voltage shift across the whole wafer.

## Finding and action

Dose Faraday cup out of calibration. Recalibrated.

## Result

Sheet resistance within 0.6 percent of target. No spatial yield pattern was involved.
