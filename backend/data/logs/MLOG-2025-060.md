# MLOG-2025-060 ETCH-03-A: Forward power unstable

Date: 2025-09-03. Chamber: ETCH-03-A.

## Symptom

Forward RF power fluctuating plus or minus 30 W; no yield impact observed yet.

## Finding and action

Generator output board fault. Generator swapped with spare.

## Result

Forward power stable at 1500 W plus or minus 4 W.
