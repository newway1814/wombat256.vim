# MLOG-2025-068 LITH-01-A: Edge bead removal misaligned

Date: 2025-10-21. Chamber: LITH-01-A.

## Symptom

Edge die failures on lots exposed on LITH-01, together with resist residue at the bevel.

## Finding and action

Edge bead removal nozzle offset by 0.4 mm. Re-centred nozzle.

## Result

Edge yield recovered. Note: this edge pattern came from litho, not etch; commonality pointed at LITH-01 at step LITHO-GATE.
