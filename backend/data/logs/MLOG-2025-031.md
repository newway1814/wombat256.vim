# MLOG-2025-031 CMP-02-A: Scratches on STI CMP wafers

Date: 2025-05-19. Chamber: CMP-02-A.

## Symptom

Inline inspection found SCRATCH defects up to 14 per wafer; wafer maps showed arc-shaped failing dies.

## Finding and action

Pad conditioner disk worn and shedding diamonds. Replaced conditioner disk.

## Result

Scratch count back below 2 per wafer after 3 monitor wafers.
