# MLOG-2026-062 ETCH-04-A: Edge ring wrong part number

Date: 2026-09-02. Chamber: ETCH-04-A.

## Symptom

After PM, edge yield dropped slightly on chamber A; reflected power normal at 12 W.

## Finding and action

Edge ring installed had the part number for a different recipe family. Replaced with the correct part.

## Result

Edge yield recovered. Lesson: an edge ring pattern with normal reflected power points at the edge ring part, not the RF match.
