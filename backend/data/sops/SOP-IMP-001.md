# SOP-IMP-001 Implant dose and beam current drift

Applies to: MC-IMP 3 implanters running well implants.

## Symptoms

- Sheet resistance on monitor wafers outside plus or minus 2 percent.
- Beam current unstable or drifting during the run.
- Parametric test failures spread across the whole wafer (not a ring or cluster), usually threshold voltage shifts.

## Checks

1. Review the dose monitor trend for the implanter.
2. Compare sheet resistance with the sister implanter.
3. Note: an implanter that simply processed many of the low-yield lots is not proof of a fault. If the yield loss has a spatial pattern such as an edge ring, an implant cause is unlikely.

## Corrective action

Recalibrate the dose Faraday cup and re-qualify on 2 monitor wafers.
