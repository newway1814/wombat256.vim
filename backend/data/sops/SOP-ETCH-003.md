# SOP-ETCH-003 Electrostatic chuck temperature and helium backside cooling

Applies to: CX-Etch 300 chambers.

## Purpose

Keep the wafer at a uniform temperature during etch. The electrostatic chuck (ESC) holds the wafer, and helium flows behind it to carry heat away.

## Symptoms of a problem

- HE-FLOW-LO alarm: helium backside flow below the limit. A single short alarm that clears itself is usually a wafer with a slight bow and needs no action.
- ESC temperature outside 60 plus or minus 2 C.
- Centre-to-edge critical dimension difference, often a centre hot spot rather than a ring.

## Checks

1. Count HE-FLOW-LO alarms over the last 7 days. Fewer than 3 transient alarms is normal.
2. Check ESC temperature in FDC; it should be flat.
3. If repeated, inspect the ESC surface for wear and the helium line for leaks.

## Corrective action

Replace the ESC only after engineering review; it is an expensive part with a long lead time.
