# SOP-ETCH-002 Etch chamber preventive maintenance (PM)

Applies to: all CX-Etch 300 chambers. Frequency: every 2,500 RF hours or quarterly.

## Scope of the PM

- Wet clean of chamber walls, liner and gas distribution plate.
- Replace the edge ring and focus ring. Confirm the part number against the tool's bill of materials.
- Inspect RF straps and connectors; replace if discoloured.
- Recalibrate the RF match network (capacitors C1 and C2) and the pressure gauge.

## Post-PM qualification

A chamber may not run product until all checks pass:

1. Leak rate below 2 mTorr per minute.
2. Seasoning: 5 dummy wafers with the production recipe.
3. Reflected RF power below 14 W on every seasoning wafer.
4. Particle check: fewer than 10 adders above 0.1 um.
5. Etch rate and uniformity on a monitor wafer within 3 percent of the pre-PM baseline.

## Known risks after PM

The most frequent post-PM failure is a mis-seated match capacitor. It often passes the first qualification but drifts over the next days as the chamber heats up. Watch the reflected power SPC chart closely for 7 days after any PM.

## Records

Log the PM in the maintenance system with technician, parts used, part numbers, and the qualification results.
