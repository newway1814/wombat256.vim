# SOP-CMP-001 CMP pad conditioning and scratch defects

Applies to: PL-400 polishers running STI CMP.

## Symptoms

- Scratch or arc-shaped lines of failing dies on wafer maps.
- Inline inspection SCRATCH defects above 5 per wafer.
- Removal rate drifting more than 5 percent from target.

## Likely causes

1. Worn pad conditioner disk shedding diamonds.
2. Pad glazing from insufficient conditioning.
3. Slurry agglomerates from an expired slurry batch.

## Corrective action

Replace the conditioner disk, re-qualify removal rate on 3 monitor wafers, and check particle counts before release.
